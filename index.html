Concept & Inspiration

📹

The Idea

It all started while watching a YouTube tutorial about making custom 3D printed LED signs. The idea of mixing digital design, 3D printing, and lighting into my own project grabbed my attention right away.

🎨

The Vision

I wanted to create my own custom lit up sign that combines text, engineering, and lighting. I used Fusion 360 for the 3D design, a 3D printer to make the parts, and LED strips for the glow.

⚡

The Goal

A fully working LED sign that can hang on a wall. It has clear letters with even lighting and a clean modern look. Something nice enough to hang in a room or workspace.

Design Process in Fusion 360

Every good sign starts with a digital design. Here is how the model came together.

Step 1

Choosing the Font

I picked a bold, modern font that would look great when lit up and have enough surface area for the light to shine through.

Step 2

Image to SVG

I converted the text into an SVG file so I could open it in Fusion 360 as a clean sketch.

Step 3

Creating Sketches

I imported the SVG into Fusion 360 and created sketches for each letter.

Step 4

Offset & Extrude

I made outlines for the LED channels, then made the base 30mm deep with 1mm thick walls.

Step 5

Diffuser Caps & Final Design

I designed snap-on diffuser caps for each letter to spread the LED light evenly across the front.

Step 6

Export STL

I exported each part as a separate STL file. One file per letter, ready for the slicer.

Key Dimensions

300 mm Width
120 mm Height
30 mm Depth
1 mm Wall Thickness

STL Files & Slicer Settings

From design to print. The settings that made it all work.

↕

Layer Height

Printed at 0.2mm layer height. This is a good balance between speed and how smooth the surface looks.

⚙

Supports Enabled

Tree supports were used for letter overhangs. They were easy to remove and left a clean surface.

🔥

PLA Filament

Standard white PLA. White is the best color for spreading LED light evenly.

■

Print Orientation

Letters were printed face-down on the build plate so the front surface comes out smooth.

★

Bambu Studio

Sliced in Bambu Studio for good control over supports, speed, and infill.

📄

STL Export Details

Each letter was exported as a separate STL file with high quality settings so the surfaces come out clean.

Areas for Improvement

Things I would change if I made another sign.

Make the Sign Larger

A bigger sign would give me more room for the LEDs and wires. It would also make putting everything together much easier.

Redesign the Diffuser Caps

The diffuser caps were hard to print and some did not fit well. I would redesign them to be simpler and more reliable.

Better Wire Management

I would add channels or a back plate to keep the wires organized and hidden. This would make the sign look cleaner.

Test Print First

Before printing the whole sign, I would test print small sections first. This would help me catch problems early.

Increase Wall Thickness

Some parts of the sign were a bit fragile. Making the walls thicker in those areas would make the sign stronger.

Improve the Mounting System

The way the sign mounts on the wall could be better. I would design a simpler way to hang it.

Use Programmable LEDs

In the future, I would use programmable RGB LEDs that allow animations and per-letter color control.

Make Letters Slightly Larger

Some letters were very small and hard to print. Making them slightly bigger would reduce printing problems.

Reflection

What worked, what didn't, and what I would do next time.

✔

What Worked Well

  • Fusion 360 helped me design the sign accurately.
  • The white PLA worked well for spreading the LED light.
  • The final sign looked clean and professional.
  • Printing the letters face-down gave a smooth finish.
⚠

Challenges

  • Printing the diffuser caps was one of the biggest challenges.
  • Some caps did not fit correctly and had to be redesigned.
  • First-layer printing problems caused failed prints.
  • Small letters were difficult to print successfully.
  • Installing LEDs inside narrow letters was difficult.
  • Routing wires through the holes took a lot of patience.
⏰

What I Would Improve

  • Make the sign larger so there is more room for LEDs and wires.
  • Make the diffuser caps easier to print and install.
  • Add better wire management channels.
  • Use programmable LEDs for more lighting effects.
  • Test print small sections before printing the entire project.
